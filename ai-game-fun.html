Ray’s Realm

A turn-based medieval strategy game. Lead the Order of the Cross against three rival armies on a map that is generated fresh every time. Over the Marne

A WWI biplane game set over the Western Front, summer 1918. Fly low over the trenches, bomb supply dumps, trains and tanks, photograph enemy positions, escort a slow bomber and hunt down an enemy ace. Dodge flak and machine-gun nests, loop back at the artillery barrage, and land at the airfield to rearm.

Single player · plays in your browser · keyboard on a computer or touch controls on a phone · sound on for the full effect.
