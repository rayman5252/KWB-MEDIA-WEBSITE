Hartford Video Production FAQ

Do you actually come to Hartford for shoots?

Yes. We're based in Somers, a short drive up I-91, and we shoot all over Hartford and the surrounding towns: offices, factories, job sites, restaurants, events and anywhere else you need a camera.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on the project, but our $899 video special is a great starting point for small businesses. Bigger productions get a custom quote after a free consultation.

Can you shoot with a drone?

Yes. We fly drones for aerials of properties, campuses, events and anything else that looks better from above.

Do you only do video?

No. Along with video production and 4K editing, we handle social media management with graphics, AI video, 2D/3D animation and marketing strategy.

Do I need to come to your office?

Nope. Most shoots happen at your place. Meetings at our Somers office are by appointment only, and consultations can happen by phone, video call or over coffee. If you like Dunkin', we'll even buy.
