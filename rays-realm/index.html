    How to play
    • Each unit has movement points per turn and can spend them in several steps until you press End Turn. Attacking ends that unit's turn. Catapults can't fire after moving.
    • Gold mines: end a move on one to seize it. Each held mine pays +2 gold at the start of your turn.
    • Income: every army earns 2 gold a turn no matter what, plus 2 for each keep and 2 for each mine it holds.
    • Barbarians: skull-banner towers hide in undiscovered land. Their riders and archers only defend their home, but they hit hard. Destroy a tower for a 10 gold bounty.
    • Recruit from the sidebar: new units appear near your keep and rest until next turn. Ships spawn on the river.
    • Ships are unarmed transports. Walk a land unit onto a ship (max 2) to board: that ends its turn and the ship's. Next turn the ship sails 3 hexes and passengers may land on any adjacent shore.
    • Forests: +1 defence, cost 2 to enter. Crags and river block foot soldiers; cross at the fords.
    • Forts: pay 30 gold to build one on any grass hex near your units. It gives +2 defence to your unit standing there, and lets you recruit one unit per turn on an adjacent hex (even a ship, if water is adjacent). It collapses if that unit is slain, and is razed if an enemy steps on it. Keeps give +2 and recruit without limit; each tower recruits one unit per turn.
    • Fog of war: every army, yours and theirs, sees only 3 hexes around its units, keeps and forts (scouts and archer towers see 4). Explored ground stays revealed, but enemies in the dark are hidden.
    • Healing: wounded units that stay out of battle recover 5% of their health each turn, or 10% if they end within 2 hexes of a keep or fort you hold.
    • Defenders counter-attack if the attacker is within their range.
    • Victory: capture all three enemy keeps by ending a move on them. Taking a keep also loots 5–25 gold. Lose your own keep — or your whole army and gold — and the realm is lost.
