    The Tale of the Realm

    War Comes

    For a hundred years Ray's Realm knew peace. The harvests were full, the river roads were open, and the great houses kept faith with the crown, from the four corners of the land to the halls beneath the mountains.

    Then the borders began to bleed. A stolen herd. A burned mill. A toll too cruel at the river bridge. Small quarrels hardened into raids, and raids into sieges, until no house trusted another.

    Now barbarian hordes gather in the hills, the gold mines are the last wealth in the land, and every keep has barred its gates. There is room for only one crown.

    To survive, you must choose your army, hold your keep, seize the mines and conquer the realm before it conquers you. Choose well.

    How to play
    • Armies: each army recruits one special unit and has its own bonus. Order of the Cross: the Paladin, and units next to a Paladin heal 3 health each turn. Dark Host: the Hydra (9 attack; replaces the Dragon) and 2 gold for every enemy unit destroyed. Golden Valkyries: the winged Valkyrie, mines that pay +1 gold, and Mercenaries for 2 gold. Emerald Court: the Elven Ranger (range 2–4), 1 extra hex of sight and 1 less damage in forest. Stonehold Clans (Dwarves): sturdier warriors, Crossbowmen instead of Archers, the Steam Tank instead of the Dragon, forts for 18 gold, 1 extra gold every turn, stone walls, and foot soldiers and ram riders that climb mountains.
    • Stone walls (Dwarves): 5 gold each, on meadow or hills within 2 hexes of a Dwarf unit or keep; 2 a turn, 12 standing, never beside an enemy or within 3 hexes of a keep the Dwarves don't hold. Enemy and barbarian ground units cannot pass; flyers go over but cannot land; Dwarves walk through. Only a Catapult, Dragon or Hydra can knock a wall down, and one hit does it.
    • Omens: from round 4 on, the sky or the land sometimes turns on everyone. Each omen is announced one round ahead (see the Omens panel). Fog Bank: everyone sees 1 hex less. Thunderstorm: ships cannot sail and ranged units lose 1 range. Hard Frost: rivers freeze for 2–3 rounds so units can walk across (ships are stuck), then the ice cracks: anyone still on it takes 4 damage and is thrown onto the nearest bank. Bountiful Harvest: mines pay double. Plague: the largest army loses 2 health on each unit that can move. Frost Giants Spotted: huge Frost Giants appear at random places, march on the nearest army and attack anyone, then retreat to the mountains after 6 rounds; slay one for 15 gold.
    • Each unit has movement points per turn and can spend them in several steps until you press End Turn. Attacking ends that unit's turn. Catapults can't fire after moving.
    • Gold mines: end a move on one to seize it. Each held mine pays +2 gold at the start of your turn.
    • Supply (army size): your army can only field as many units as your land can feed: 6, plus 4 for each keep, 1 for each gold mine and 2 for each fort you hold. Every unit counts, including passengers and archer towers, but Mercenaries don't. The ⚔ counter shows units fielded / supply. At the cap you can still hire Mercenaries, but can't recruit anyone else until you take more land or lose units, so grabbing mines and keeps (and razing the enemy's) matters more than a big pile of gold.
    • Zone of control: a foot or mounted unit that steps next to an enemy it can see must stop there for the turn. Hold a line and the enemy can't just walk past it. Flyers, ships and Scouts ignore zones of control, and a unit that starts its turn engaged may still step away.
    • Income: every army earns 2 gold a turn no matter what, plus 2 for each keep and 2 for each mine it holds.
    • Barbarians: skull-banner towers hide in undiscovered land. Their riders and archers only defend their home, but they hit hard. Destroy a tower for a 10 gold bounty.
    • Recruit from the sidebar: new units appear near your keep and rest until next turn. Each keep you hold lets you recruit up to 4 units per turn, and each fort can also recruit two more units beside it. Ships spawn on the river.
    • Mercenaries cost 3 gold and match a Foot Soldier. They are hired on the spot: place one on any hex you can see, not just near a keep. A contract lasts 5 rounds (a badge shows the rounds left), or until he is killed. Three at a time at most. They don't use up your keep recruits or count towards your supply.
    • Ships are unarmed transports that carry 2 land units. Walk a unit onto a ship to board, sail up to 4 hexes, then select the ship and press Unload to land passengers on any adjacent shore, all in the same turn. Boarding uses up the unit's walking, and landing ends its turn (no attack after landing). Unload all lands everyone at once, each on a free shore hex. Archers, Crossbowmen and Rangers can fire from the deck (a unit that has fired stays aboard until next turn), and when a ship is attacked and stays afloat, every one of them shoots back, however close or far the attacker is. Steam Tanks are too heavy to board.
    • Forests: +1 defence, cost 2 to enter (free for the Emerald Court). Hills: +1 defence and +1 sight for a foot unit standing on them (flyers get neither). Crags and river block foot soldiers; cross at the fords. Dwarf warriors and ram riders can climb crags (+2 defence), one crag hex per turn.
    • Forts: pay 25 gold to build one on any grass hex near your units. It gives +2 defence to your unit standing there, +2 supply, and lets you recruit two units per turn on adjacent hexes (even a ship, if water is adjacent). It collapses if that unit is slain, and is razed if an enemy steps on it. Keeps give +2 and recruit without limit; each fort recruits two units per turn.
    • Scriptorium (long games): raise one on any open hex you can see (30 gold) to research technologies: armour, whetstones, dirigibles and a secret of your own army. Each discovery is paid for up front and lands at a random time, and a fire can destroy the work. If an enemy ends a move on your Scriptorium it is captured: you lose your newest discovery and the capturer learns one.
    • Mana & Wizards: each keep you hold gives 1 mana a turn, each mana crystal mine you hold gives 2 (seize them like gold mines), and a Scriptorium gives 1. Mana pays for research and for Wizard spells, which a Wizard casts instead of attacking: Heal (3), Flash (6) stuns an enemy, and Chain Lightning (9) leaps between up to four enemies. A Wizard beside a crystal mine mends 2 health a turn.
    • Fog of war: every army, yours and theirs, sees only 3 hexes around its units, keeps and forts (scouts and archer towers see 4). Explored ground stays revealed, but enemies in the dark are hidden.
    • Healing: wounded units that stay out of battle recover 5% of their health each turn, or 10% if they end within 2 hexes of a keep or fort you hold.
    • Defenders counter-attack if the attacker is within their range.
    • Saving: the game saves itself at the start of each of your turns and when you close the page, and you can press Save (or S) any time on your turn. Use Continue on this screen to pick up where you left off. There is one save slot per browser; it is cleared when a battle is won or lost.
    • Enemy armies: choose to face 1, 2 or 3 computer armies before a New Battle; they are drawn at random from the other armies, four armies at most in a battle. Any empty corner keeps are abandoned: march in to claim them for loot and income (the enemy may grab them too).
    • Head to Head Online: play a friend on another computer. One of you picks an army and creates a match, then texts the 5-letter code; the other joins and picks a different army. Only your two armies are on the map, with twice the barbarians. Each turn is sent automatically when you press End Turn. If the connection drops, the guest reconnects on their own; the host can use Resume match.
    • Victory: capture every enemy keep by ending a move on it. Taking a keep also loots 5–25 gold. Lose your own keep — or your whole army and gold — and the realm is lost.
