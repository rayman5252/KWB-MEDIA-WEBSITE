Our Video Portfolio

Charity – United Way

Helping charities and non-profits is very near and dear to our hearts. When the United Way of Northern CT reached out to us, not just for a video, but to organize an entire event to create their marketing, we jumped at the chance.

Food and Restaurant

Newman's Own is a brand almost everyone knows about. They make great products and support wonderful charities. KWB was lucky enough to help the great folks from Newman's Own with their line of take out pizzas.

Non-Profit – Mark Twain House

Owner Ray Wolters has a soft spot for all things historical. When Ray and his team found out the Mark Twain House didn't have the funds to promote their live character tours, they sprung into action — with no budget and only two hours to shoot before start of business.

Animation – LCA

Lice Clinics of America wanted to create an engaging animated character to represent their brand. KWB started with hand drawn sketches and created "Lenny" and his family, who have since appeared on coloring books and product labels.

Broadcast – CT Tourism

Connecticut has some great places to celebrate tourism, but you still need to tell people about them. Crews drove around the state getting hours of footage, rushed back to home base and distilled it down to a :30 second commercial.

Recreation

Working with friends at another marketing company, KWB Media was tasked with shooting and editing this piece for the go kart track at Foxwoods.

Automotive

KWB Media got a chance to play with some cool cars in upstate New York, making an on-the-spot commercial to push sports cars — having staff who know their cars certainly helps.

Corporate/Industrial

Whether it's a local photography studio or a Fortune 500 company, we can handle any level of corporate video. Internal marketing for big groups like Cigna, PepsiCo, and BHI Energy are our bread and butter.
