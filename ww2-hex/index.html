    Hex Command: WWII

    Germany holds mainland Europe. The free world has one move left. Pick your nation, build your forces and take the enemy capitals before they take yours.
    How to play
    • Goal: capture the capital of every enemy nation. Your allies are AI commanders. Lose your own capital and you are out.
    • Turns: USA, UK, USSR, Germany/Italy, then Japan. Click a unit, click a blue hex to move, click a red hex to attack. End Turn when done.
    • Production: click one of your cities to buy units with Industry Points (IP). Each city makes 1 unit per turn, capitals make 2. Ships need a coastal city. Cities pay IP every turn, so capture more of them.
    • Land: infantry are cheap and take cities. Armor hits hard but is slow in forest and mountains. Units in forests, mountains and cities defend better.
    • Sea: transports carry 2 land units (move a land unit onto an adjacent transport, then sail and land on the coast). Battleships hit up to 2 hexes away. Submarines are hidden until something gets next to them and are deadly to ships.
    • Air: aircraft take off from cities and carriers, and must land again within 2 turns (bombers) or 1 turn after the sortie (fighters) or they crash. Fighters hunt aircraft, bombers smash ground and ships.
    • Fog of war: you and your allies only see what your units and cities can see.
