    Landfall: WWII

    The world at war, 1940. Pick your map, pick your nation, build your forces and take the enemy capitals before they take yours.
    How to play
    • Goal: capture the capital of every enemy nation. Your allies are AI commanders. Lose your own capital and you are out.
    • Turns: USA, UK, USSR, Germany/Italy, then Japan. Click a unit stack, click a white-outlined territory to move, click a red-hatched one to attack. Use the + and − buttons to move only part of a stack. End Turn when done.
    • The map: the world is divided into territories and sea zones. It wraps: sail or fly off the right edge and you come out on the left. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, arrow keys to scroll.
    • Production: click one of your territories, or press the Buy button, to buy units with Industry Points (IP). Richer territories build more units per turn, capitals build the most. Ships appear in a sea zone next to a coastal territory. Every territory you hold pays its IP value each turn, so capture more of them.
    • Land: infantry are cheap and take territory. Armor hits hard and moves two territories. Units in forests, mountains, rich territories and capitals defend better. Move into an empty enemy or neutral territory to take it.
    • Sea: transports carry 3 land units (move a land unit into the sea zone holding your transport, then sail and unload onto an adjacent coast). Battleships hit up to 2 zones away. Submarines are hidden until something gets next to them and are deadly to ships.
    • Air: aircraft take off from your territories and carriers, and must land again within 2 turns (bombers) or 1 turn after the sortie (fighters) or they crash. Fighters hunt aircraft, bombers smash ground and ships.
    • Fog of war: you and your allies only see what your units and cities can see.
