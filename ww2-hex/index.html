    Landfall: WWII

    The world at war, 1940. Pick your map, pick your nation, build your forces and take the enemy capitals before they take yours.
    How to play
    • Whose is whose: the base under every unit is painted in its nation’s colour, matching the list beside the map.
    • Goal: capture the capital of every enemy nation. Your allies are AI commanders. Lose your own capital and you are out.
    • Turns: the nation you command moves first each round, then the others follow in the order USA, UK, USSR, Germany/Italy, Japan. Click a unit stack to select it, then drag it onto a white-outlined territory to move, or onto a red-hatched one to attack. While you drag, the piece you picked up turns grey where it started and an arrow shows where it is headed: white to move, red to attack, grey if it can’t go there. A click never moves anything; Esc or right-click cancels a drag. Use the + and − buttons to move only part of a stack. End Turn when done.
    • The map: the world is divided into territories and sea zones. It wraps: sail or fly off the right edge and you come out on the left. Drag the map to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, arrow keys to scroll.
    • Production: units can only be produced at a factory. Every capital starts with one, and each nation has a second in another major territory. Click a factory, or press the Buy button, to buy units with Industry Points (IP). Ships appear in a sea zone next to the factory’s coast. Every territory you hold pays its IP value each turn, so capture more of them.
    • New factories: click any territory you own that has none and buy one for 15 IP. It makes up to 2 units a turn, starting on your next turn. Capture an enemy territory and its factory is yours from your next turn.
    • Land: infantry are cheap and take territory. Armor hits hard and moves two territories. Units in forests, mountains, rich territories and capitals defend better. Move into an empty enemy or neutral territory to take it.
    • Sea: transports carry 3 land units (drag a land unit into the sea zone holding your transport, then sail and unload onto an adjacent coast). Battleships hit up to 2 zones away. Submarines are hidden until something gets next to them and are deadly to ships.
    • Air: aircraft take off from your territories and carriers, and must land again within 2 turns (bombers) or 1 turn after the sortie (fighters) or they crash. Fighters hunt aircraft, bombers smash ground and ships.
    • Fog of war: you and your allies only see what your units and cities can see.
